CyberNest Holdings LLC [CORP]

CyberNest Holdings LLC is a manager-managed operating limited liability company organized in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. The company provides technical field engineering services and develops proprietary software, security technologies, and research outputs. CyberNest Holdings LLC is an operating company and not a passive investment fund.

Field Engineering Services: On-site technical dispatches executed with company-supplied tools and bulk inventory, verified through arrival and completion site documentation and logged into a centralized digital ledger.

Dept: CyberNest Labs [R&D]

CyberNest Labs is the internal R&D and security software department of CyberNest Holdings LLC. It builds offline-first, air-gapped security, network configuration, peer-to-peer communication, and system diagnostic tools using Rust, Python, Go, POSIX Shell, and HTMX/Textual:

  • BlackRoute-Web: Web dynamic application security testing (DAST) and attack surface auditor.
  • KwikLogPro: Cross-platform (Pop!_OS Linux & Windows) system health and log regression auditor.
  • NestComm: Peer-to-peer encrypted synchronous and store-and-forward messaging node.
  • NestLink: Peer-to-peer SSH tunneling and NAT traversal utility.
  • NestPatch: Single-IP automated VLAN provisioner and network switch hardening terminal tool.
  • NestSBOM: Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) ingestion and CycloneDX/SPDX generator.
