## 1. Formal Attestation of 100% Domestic Governance, Assets & Control

In accordance with the *CyberNest Holdings LLC Master Corporate Charter & Treasury Policy (v1.0.2)*, the *Operating Agreement of CyberNest Holdings LLC (v1.0.2)*, and the *Member Capital Ledger Policy (v1.0.2)*, **CyberNest Holdings LLC** hereby formally attests and certifies that:

1. **100% Domestic Legal Entity & Exclusive Asset Ownership**:
   * **CyberNest Holdings LLC** is a manager-managed operating limited liability company organized and governed under the laws of the **Commonwealth of Pennsylvania**.
   * Pursuant to Article I, Article II (Section 2.3), Article V (Section 5.1), and Article IX of the Operating Agreement, **CyberNest Holdings LLC owns 100% of all intellectual property, operating assets, proprietary software, security technologies, and centralized corporate treasury capital**.
   * CyberNest Holdings LLC is an operating company and is **not** an investment fund, pooled investment vehicle, or passive investment entity.
2. **100% Domestic U.S. Citizen Executive Management**:
   * Management authority of CyberNest Holdings LLC is vested exclusively in its domestic managers (Article VII of the Operating Agreement):
     * **William R. M. (Auxk0rd)** - **President and Chief Architect** (Initial Member; U.S. Citizen): Authority over R&D, technical operations, systems architecture, security, treasury management, investment strategy, risk controls, banking relationships, and Capital Units valuation.
     * **James D. J. (D)** - **Chief Executive Officer** (Initial Member; U.S. Citizen): Authority over sales, public outreach, growth strategy, partnerships, member coordination, operational execution, client/contract acquisition, and public representation.
3. **Nature of Capital Units & Zero Foreign Ownership or Voting Control**:
   * Under Article III of the Operating Agreement and Section 3 of the Member Capital Ledger Policy, **Capital Units represent internal economic participation only** and are **not** equity interests, do **not** convey voting rights, do **not** convey ownership of Company property or intellectual property, and do **not** create creditor status.
   * The total `100,000` outstanding Capital Units recorded in the authoritative Member Capital Ledger are allocated exclusively among the two domestic initial Members and three internal Company departments:
     * **William R. M.**: `40,000 Units` (`40%`)
     * **James D. J.**: `37,000 Units` (`37%`)
     * **CyberNest Labs** (Internal Department): `10,000 Units` (`10%`)
     * **CyberNest Farms** (Internal Department): `10,000 Units` (`10%`)
     * **CyberNest ADMD** (Internal Artistry Design and Marketing Department): `3,000 Units` (`3%`)
   * Per Article VIII of the Operating Agreement and Section 5 of the Member Capital Ledger Policy, **CyberNest Labs**, **CyberNest Farms**, and **CyberNest ADMD** are internal departments of CyberNest Holdings LLC for accounting purposes only and are **not** separate legal entities.
4. **Zero Foreign Control or Influence**:
   * No foreign government, foreign national, foreign corporation, or non-U.S. person holds any governance authority, management seat, Capital Units, debt instrument, or beneficial control over CyberNest Holdings LLC.
5. **100% Domestic Intellectual Property & Air-Gapped Software Provenance**:
   * All software applications developed by the CyberNest Labs department for CyberNest Holdings LLC (`BlackRoute-Web`, `KwikLogPro`, `NestComm`, `NestLink`, `NestPatch`, `NestSBOM`) are architected, written, compiled, and verified on domestic U.S. workstations.
   * All applications operate 100% offline/air-gapped on local loopback (`127.0.0.1`) with zero foreign cloud dependencies, zero external runtime dependencies, and signed CycloneDX 1.5 / SPDX 2.3 Software Bills of Materials (SBOMs).
