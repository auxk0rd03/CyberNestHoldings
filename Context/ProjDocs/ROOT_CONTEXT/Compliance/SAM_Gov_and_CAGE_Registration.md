## 3. SAM.gov Registration Checklist & Onboarding Sequence

To transition from `PRE-REGISTRATION` to active federal prime/subcontractor eligibility, CyberNest Holdings LLC executes the following sequence:

1. **Step 1 - SAM.gov Entity Validation**: Submit Pennsylvania Department of State LLC formation records and IRS EIN confirmation to SAM.gov to receive the official 12-character **Unique Entity Identifier (UEI)**.
2. **Step 2 - Financial & Treasury Linkage**: Link the centralized CyberNest Holdings LLC corporate banking account (governed by the 6-month operating expense liquidity reserve rule in the Master Charter) for Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) and execute the notarized SAM.gov Entity Administrator designation.
3. **Step 3 - DLA CAGE Assignment**: Complete representations and certifications (FAR/DFARS) in SAM.gov for automatic **CAGE Code** assignment by the Defense Logistics Agency.
4. **Step 4 - PIEE / SPRS Submission**: Register the assigned UEI and CAGE Code in the DoD Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment (PIEE) and upload the NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 Basic Self-Assessment (`SPRS_Assessment_Score_Report.md`).
