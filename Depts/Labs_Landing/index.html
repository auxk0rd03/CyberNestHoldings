About CyberNest Labs [R&D]

CyberNest Labs is the internal research and software engineering department of CyberNest Holdings LLC. The department builds local-first, air-gapped security, network provisioning, peer-to-peer communication, and system diagnostic tools engineered with Rust, Python, Go, POSIX Shell, and HTMX/Textual.

All software runs locally on loopback (127.0.0.1) without external cloud or CDN dependencies.
